#### Hot Fix - 07/18

Item Changes

- Crystal of Sandstorms has been adjusted to be tradeable and sellable. You can no longer store this item in your personal or guild banks.

Scenarios

- Guard agro range has been adjusted in Khaine’s Embrace and Serpents Passage to bring them in line with other scenario guard behavior.
