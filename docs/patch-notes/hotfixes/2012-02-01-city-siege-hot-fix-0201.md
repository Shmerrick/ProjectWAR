# City Siege Hot Fix - 02/01

Official Mythic hot fix notes, published 2012-02-01.
Retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of
`http://www.warhammeronline.com/article/City-Siege-Hot-Fix-0201`.
Converted from HTML to plain text; wording, ordering and spelling are unchanged.

As part of the introduction of Fortresses, we introduced a mechanic to allow City Queuing via the scenario UI element. Unfortunately, this has led to some issues with the City invasion instances within the Live environment that were not discovered previously. Because of this, we need to remove this functionality, at least for the moment to ensure that players can queue and enjoy the City Instances properly.

This will mean players will need to enter the city via the doors to the city as they did previously.
