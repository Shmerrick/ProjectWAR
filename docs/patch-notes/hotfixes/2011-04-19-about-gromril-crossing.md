# About Gromril Crossing

Official Mythic hot fix notes, published 2011-04-19.
Retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of
`http://www.warhammeronline.com/article/About-Gromril-Crossing`.
Converted from HTML to plain text; wording, ordering and spelling are unchanged.

Greetings,

We’ve encountered and fixed an issue with Gromril Crossing where the scenario was not rewarding players with tokens upon completion. To thank you for the quick reports and to make up a bit for any tokens that might have been lost, we’ve decided to put up a special bonus for the scenario this weekend.

Look to the Herald for further details on Friday.
