# Hot Fix - 02/06

Official Mythic hot fix notes, published 2012-02-06.
Retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of
`http://www.warhammeronline.com/article/Hot-Fix-0206`.
Converted from HTML to plain text; wording, ordering and spelling are unchanged.

To address an issue with the way players get to contested cities the following fix was applied:

- While the Cities are contested, there is now a Portal available to teleport players closer to the city and a return-trip Portal to teleport players back. These are located in the Fortress zones, near the spawn location of the attacking Realm’s siege equipment. The return-trip Portal is near the gates and both portals will disappear when the City is no longer contested.
