# Patch 1.4.8 Arrives on PTS

Mythic public-test-server announcement, published 2012-11-20.
Retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of
`http://www.warhammeronline.com/article/Patch-148-Arrives-PTS`.
Converted from HTML to plain text; wording, ordering and spelling are unchanged.

Patch 1.4.8 comes to the Public Test Server and it will remain open from 11/21 through 11/28. Here are some of the highlights from the patch:

- Currency Conversion: There are War Crest Vault Keepers located in the T1 Warcamps in EvC, as well as in the Capital Cities near the renown gear vendors.

- The Supplier Quartermasters in all T1-T3 Warcamps has a new quest that will appear if you are the appropriate level for that tier.

- Renown Vendors have had their contents shuffled a bit to both make things a little easier to find, and bring them in line with the new currency.

- And most importantly of all: Fight and claim War Crests!!

Remember to transfer over a few characters of various levels to check out all of the changes and enjoy experimenting with the new currency in each tier. For more details regarding the changes check out the post Keaven Freeman, the War Design & Implementation Lead, has put up in the Dev Discussions forum.
