# College of Corruption Test on PTS - 06/16

Mythic public-test-server announcement, published 2011-06-14.
Retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of
`http://www.warhammeronline.com/article/College-Corruption-Test-PTS-0616`.
Converted from HTML to plain text; wording, ordering and spelling are unchanged.

Greetings,

On Thursday, 06/16, we invite you to join us in a test of the new event scenario, the College of Corruption. There will be two session of about one hour at 2 pm EDT (20:00 CEST) and 7 pm EDT (Friday morning 1:00 CEST) during which we open the College for you.

If you want to help and participate, please have your template character ready and waiting in your capital city before the start time. Feel free to organize groups as you like.

Apart from feedback about the general playability, we want to gather information about the optimal size (6vs6 or 12vs12), the Aspects of Fire, etc. You will also find our topics in a dedicated feedback thread in the Dev Discussion area which we’ll open on Thursday.

As a side note: Some of you might remember that we have announced the start of the Live Event for June 21st in the latest Community Update. But we want to have enough time on PTS and therefore postpone the Event (and the patch day) to the week around the 29th.

We’ll see in college!
