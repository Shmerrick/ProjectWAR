## Not Bugs (investigated and explained)

### Correction, 2026-09-05 -- the word "junk", and a deletion that should not have happened

The first revision of migration 47 *deleted* the 1,842 unresolvable Type-2 public-quest spawn rows and
described them as junk "duplicated debris on top" of the real spawns. The duplication claim was rechecked
against the untouched base dump (`Database/war_world.7z`, table `pquest_spawns`, 29,437 rows) and is **false**:
measured against the same objective's surviving Type-1 rows, only 34 of the 1,842 lie within 50 units, 168
within 300, and 1,640 further than 300 away. Their positions are their own, and a position plus a zone is
exactly what identified 39 other prototypes against the captures in migrations 38, 41, 43, 46 and 48.

Migration 47 was rewritten to move such rows into `pquest_spawns_unresolved` instead of deleting them, and
migration 49 restores the archive contents verbatim from the base dump on any database where the deleting
revision had already run. Both were applied twice to the local Release database; the archive holds 1,842 rows
and no row leaked back into the live table.

What remains true of those rows, and is stated in the migrations rather than summarised as "junk":
`PQuestObjective.Reset` logs the missing prototype and skips them, so they spawn nothing; a Type-2 row cannot
credit a kill objective; none of them names its own objective's kill target; and every affected objective
keeps its full Type-1 creature set.
