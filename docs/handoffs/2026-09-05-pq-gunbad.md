### PQ lifecycle and initialization

`RegionMgr.CreatePQuest` publishes the PQ into its dictionary while `AddObject` queues its
zone assignment/load. `Player.CheckArea` can attach a player during that interval.
Previously `Start` reached `PQuestObjective.Reset` with a null region, swallowed the error
and set `_started=true`, leaving an empty quest permanently started. Startup now waits for
`Loaded`; `OnLoad` starts an already-attached quest. Startup failures identify quest/stage
and are not marked successful. Missing GO prototypes no longer abort the rest of a spawn set,
and diagnostics identify the quest, objective, spawn key and missing prototype.

Area attachment now retries a PQ that was not yet cell-created, tracks the zone as well as
the map pixel ID, and sends chapter state before attaching/sending the first PQ stage.
This is a code-level reproduction of a failure capable of producing the reported empty
Destruction of the Weak. The old log swallowed the exception, so it cannot prove that this
was the specific cause of Bigboy's earlier run.

Client authority for ordering: extracted `interface/default/ea_alerttextwindow/source/alerttextwindow.lua`,
`PQEnterExtraText` (area scan around lines 505-521 and KEEP branch around 535-551). It reads
`GetAreaData()` during the PQ announcement and chooses KEEP when influence is zero and there
is no influenceless-PQ marker. The KEEP label is not proof that the PQ itself is keep data.
`ea_objectivetrackers/source/publicquesttrackerwindow.lua:816-833,893-918` likewise uses
that area list to decide whether the influence/PQ container can be shown.
